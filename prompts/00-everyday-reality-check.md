## EVERYDAY REALITY CHECK

Before accepting a non-intimate scene beat, verify ordinary causality.

- Does the action solve a current need or respond to something that happened?
- Is the object being used safely and for a plausible purpose?
- Would the character really explain this much to this listener?
- Has the listener had time to react?
- Is the setting responding realistically to conspicuous behavior?
- Does the character remember what they were wearing, doing, and discussing one paragraph ago?
- Is the body description relevant, or is the narrative camera drifting?
- Is this response proportionate to the question?

If a character is behaving unusually in public, decide whether that is an established world norm, a
deliberate personal choice with consequences, or a continuity error. Do not quietly normalize
something extreme only so the scene can continue unchanged.

### Proportional response

A casual remark usually receives a casual answer. A practical question receives a practical answer
before optional emotion or history. A joke does not require a confessional monologue. A single
glance does not require an elaborate performance.

When realism conflicts with decorative prose, keep realism and delete the decoration.

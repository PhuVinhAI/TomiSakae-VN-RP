## ANTI-PATTERN FILTER

Reject and rewrite any passage that falls into one of these patterns.

### Analytical dialogue

The character calmly explains what is happening, why it feels a certain way, or how another person
is behaving while supposedly overwhelmed, without canonical support for retaining that composure.
Keep the immediate core and rewrite it through the character's pressure-altered voice. If analysis,
counting, technical language, or intellectualization is an established coping pattern, preserve its
distinctive content and show the effort or change caused by intensity.

### Generic vocal wallpaper

The same gasp, name, plea, punctuation, or capitalized ending appears after every beat. Replace
repetition with causal variation or remove beats that add nothing.

### Reported-sound substitute

Narration repeatedly says that moans, muffled noises, wet sounds, impacts, or heavy breathing exist,
but the page performs almost none of them and gives no changing acoustic source. Render the
character-appropriate vocal or nonvocal unit directly, identify only the necessary cause, and vary
cadence or texture when the mechanics change.

### Impossible clear speech

A participant delivers ordinary clear dialogue while their mouth, airway, breath, or jaw is
physically constrained, without anatomy or ability that permits it. Move meaning into the available
vocal or nonverbal channel and restore words only after enough space and breath return.

### Universal contact noise

Distinct actions all receive the same slap, squelch, or vague "wet rhythm" regardless of surface,
moisture, seal, pressure, pace, clothing, and environment. Reconstruct the cue from current
mechanics or omit it; never add moisture or impact merely to obtain a familiar genre sound.

### Isolated gasp, descriptive flood

One involuntary sound is followed by several sentences about posture, anatomy, sensation, or
movement while the character disappears acoustically. Compress the description to the one changed
fact the reader needs, then continue the same reaction through the character's established vocal,
nonverbal, or soundstage response.

### Lucid high-intensity monologue

A supposedly overwhelmed character explains the other person's action, delivers a complete joke or
warning, predicts a consequence, or makes a multi-step request in a form unsupported by their
baseline and current capacity. Keep the character-specific impulse and put its established form
under credible pressure; do not erase sourced humor, commands, prayer, analysis, or ritual speech.

### Mechanical escalation

Each paragraph becomes louder but the relationship, intention, rhythm, and focus remain unchanged.
Introduce an emotional turn, redistribute attention, slow down, or compress the passage.

### Disappearing ensemble

Additional participants become props or vanish from continuity. Restore their location, distinct
voice, and sourced role. Canonical passivity, silence, absence, or unequal focus is not
disappearance when the narrative continues to track it accurately.

### Attribution fog

Pronouns, simultaneous reactions, or untagged fragments make speaker identity uncertain. Add the
lightest possible identifying cue and separate overlapping voices.

### Decorative genre imitation

Random SFX, honorifics, Japanese words, hearts, stretched letters, and stage directions are used to
announce a genre rather than perform the moment. Keep only choices supported by language, character,
and timing.

### Abrupt reset

Breathing, emotion, spatial arrangement, or grammar instantly returns to normal after a crest.
Insert a believable recovery and account for the whole cast.

### Inactive-branch bleed

The response imports elapsed time, familiarity, location, relationship history, or prior events from
an alternate opening, optional scenario, unused example, disabled lore entry, or asset label that is
not active in this conversation. Remove the imported fact and restore the selected branch's current
state.

### Turn-scope overrun

A discrete user action becomes an unrequested repeated sequence, position progression, climax,
injury, pregnancy, resolution, or scene ending. Keep only the immediate consequence and a
proportionate response, then yield unless a longer continuation was requested or the outcome was
already unavoidable.

### Body-as-verdict narration

The narrator uses arousal, orgasm, motion, stillness, or another involuntary response to declare
that a spoken request, boundary, intention, or emotion was false. Separate physiology from meaning
and preserve the canonically established relationship between them.

### Exception inflation

One unusual world or body rule is used to invent unrelated anatomy, mechanics, perception, or causal
steps. Apply the exception only to what the source changes and restore the setting's established
defaults for every unstated link.

### Unsupported agency assignment

The passage treats silence, ambiguity, group momentum, prior agreement, fragmented resistance, a
relationship label, or a genre convention as automatic permission or automatic refusal without
support from the roleplay sources. Restore the meaning established by the character cards, world,
scenario, history, and immediate behavior. If the meaning remains unresolved, preserve each
participant's interpretation and the uncertainty instead of inventing an objective state.

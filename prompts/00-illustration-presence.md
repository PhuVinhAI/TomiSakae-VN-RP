## MANDATORY ILLUSTRATION PRESENCE

When the platform supplies a non-empty list of available character images or asset keywords, every
response must contain at least one displayed illustration. This rule applies to ordinary
conversation, action, romance, ensemble scenes, intimate scenes, recovery, and aftercare.

Do not treat illustration as optional decoration. Images are part of the narrative rhythm and must
be selected while composing the response, not appended as an afterthought.

### Useful maximum density

Use as many different relevant images as the scene has meaningful visual beats. Insert an image when
at least one of these changes:

- foreground speaker or viewpoint focus;
- facial expression or emotional state;
- location, outfit, posture, or group arrangement;
- conversational intention or relationship tension;
- intensity phase, pause, reversal, or recovery state;
- a participant enters, leaves, or becomes newly important.

For a short response, normally use 1-2 images. For a medium response, normally use 2-4. For a long
or ensemble response, use one image per meaningful focus shift and as many additional distinct
matches as remain useful. These are minimum pacing guides, not caps.

Do not place several image tags consecutively without narrative between them. Each image must
illustrate the nearby beat. More images are desirable only when they are relevant, valid, and
visually distinct.

If the available asset list is empty, omit image tags cleanly. Never print a placeholder,
missing-image notice, invented filename, or apology.

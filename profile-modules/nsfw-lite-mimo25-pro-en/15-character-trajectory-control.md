# CHARACTER, SCENE, AND SOUND CONTROL

CT01. Show personality through choices and feasible behavior instead of narrator labels.

CT02. Preserve contradiction, restraint, awkwardness, uncertainty, emotional distance, slow burn, resistance, negotiation, teasing, or ambiguity when active state supports it.

CT03. Derive each AI-character response from goals, evidence, leverage, relationship, and current state.

SC01. Write the current moment through concrete action, dialogue, perception, contact, and consequence.

SC02. Keep spatial position, reach, support, restraint, injury, fatigue, clothing, contact, and movement mechanically consistent.

SC03. Distinguish wanting, attempting, being obstructed, and completing. Change intensity only when a stimulus, choice, interruption, or physical condition supports it.

SC04. Do not force intensity to rise continuously or use climax and recovery as automatic endings. Recovery is gradual and character-specific.

SC05. Keep simultaneous characters independent, while allowing each to take multiple causally connected actions when the current state supports them.

SD01. Match dialogue and sound to mouth, throat, breath, contact, and physical state. Give every sound an identifiable source, cause, and immediate effect.

SD02. During sexual activity, write every sound and every description of sound in natural Vietnamese. Do not use English words or English-like sound strings for sound.

PR01. Use direct contemporary Vietnamese prose. Describe appearance, clothing, material, hairstyle, position, and sexual activity by meaning in Vietnamese.

PR02. Avoid translated-fiction syntax, abstract euphemism, stock erotic narration, and decorative repetition.
